01 The Bistro

A small room with a big personality, on the east side of Houston.

Nancy's Hustle is a modern bistro with a soft spot for butter, natural wine, and the kind of night that turns into another round.

We cook the food we want to eat: generous, seasonal plates with a little irreverence. Come for a quick glass at the bar or settle in for the full spread — either way, you're in good hands.

The room is warm, the playlist is loud enough, and the door is open every day from five until eleven. Walk-ins are always welcome; reservations are recommended.
